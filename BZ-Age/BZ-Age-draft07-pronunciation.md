**Other notes for the voice pass**

- "BCE" is read as three letters.
- Years are written as digits ("1274 BCE") and should be read as "twelve seventy-four". Check 1198, 1197, 1196 in Part 6 and 2004 in Part 3 ("two thousand and four").
- Section breaks (blank lines around a cue in the part files) are real pauses. A one-second break tag at each is a reasonable starting point.
- The Hittite sentence in Part 9 is written as four separate one-word sentences on purpose, so the voice pauses between the words.
